About

JH

I'm a full-stack developer who works across the entire product — from interface design down to the database. Most of what I build starts as a "what if": what if getting dressed came with a weather forecast, what if a scheduling headache could just disappear. I follow those questions all the way to a finished, production-ready build.

My background spans front-end and back-end development, database design, application security, and full-stack project delivery, paired with formal training in interaction design, usability, and product management — so I design with the same rigor I code with. An app should feel obvious to use before it feels impressive.

Outside of that I'm usually planning a hike or a trip, and yes, a certain pink, paint-loving panther has appointed himself creative director of this page.
